What each part really is

🔑
The shop key = logging in

Only that client's people can open their showroom. No key, no entry.

🛋️
Their own stock = their own catalog

Each showroom holds only that client's furniture and fabrics. Client A never sees Client B's sofa. This is the whole promise of the product.

🔒
Two locks = nobody enters another's shop

One lock on the shop door, one on the storage room downstairs. Even if one ever failed, the other still holds. A client's data simply cannot leak to another.

🧰
We set up the shop = onboarding

A new client just hands us photos of their furniture and a fabric list. Our team builds the 3D models and marks what's customizable. The client does nothing technical.

💡
The premium lighting = the 3D engine

The magic that makes furniture look real and beautiful already exists. Every showroom gets it — we don't rebuild it.

♻️
Security desk & billing counter = already there

The building already has an ID system and a billing counter (from Livinit's other products). We reuse them — so there's less to build and we launch faster.

Why this is good for the business

1
Adding a client is easy

A new client = opening a new showroom. Repeatable and fast — no engineer needed each time.

2
Every client's data is safe

Fully separated. A competitor's furniture can never leak into another client's view.

3
We launch faster

The lighting, the login, the billing already exist. We just build the showrooms on top.

🛡️

One honest note: a real shop lock can be broken — here the security is far stronger, closer to a bank locker. The mall is just to picture it; the real protection is much tougher.
